    How the estimate works

    There is no public live feed of Cairo Metro ridership, so this prototype builds the estimate from patterns people already know: the morning and afternoon rush, the Egyptian weekend on Friday and Saturday, interchanges that pull crowds from two lines, and campuses that empty out at 3 PM.

    crowd = time-of-day curve × station pull × rush direction × line factor

    Prototype. Scores come from a rule-based model, not live counts. The next version would take quick "how full is it?" check-ins from riders and blend them into these curves.
